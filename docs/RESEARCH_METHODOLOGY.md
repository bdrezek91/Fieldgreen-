# Research Methodology

Status: architecture baseline; implementation begins in later phases.

Research must be hypothesis-led, reproducible and falsifiable. Optimization data and
evaluation data remain separate. Final evidence is chronological out-of-sample performance
after realistic costs, compared with benchmarks including Random Entry under equivalent risk.

Detailed walk-forward, robustness, Monte Carlo and multiple-testing requirements are in
[`PHASE_0_ARCHITECTURE_RESEARCH.md`](PHASE_0_ARCHITECTURE_RESEARCH.md).
